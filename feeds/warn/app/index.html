What's a WARN notice?

Under the federal WARN Act, employers with 100+ workers must give 60 days' notice before a plant closing or mass layoff, and file it with the state. States publish these notices, and WARN Watch collects them in one place.

How often is this updated?

  • WARN Watch checks each state's official list every few hours.
  • States post new notices on their own schedules, usually a few times a week and often several days after the employer filed. New Jersey only lists the month a notice was posted.
  • While you have this page open, it refreshes itself every minute, so new notices appear without reloading.
